Integration of Advanced Systems in Environmental Science and Water Desalination (CRC Press) · 2025

Blockchain Architecture for Healthcare IoT Systems: An Architectural Comparison

Zouhair Elhadari, Hicham Zougagh, Noureddine Idboufker, Mohamed Ech-Chebaby

This chapter compares blockchain architectures for healthcare IoT, outlining design trade-offs across scalability, privacy, and interoperability.

Contribution. We compare architectural patterns and map them to healthcare IoT constraints and regulatory needs.

Highlights. The analysis informs design choices for secure, interoperable healthcare systems.

Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems (Springer) · 2025

Enhanced ECC-Based Authentication Protocol for IoT Devices M2C

Mohamed Ech-Chebaby, Hicham Zougagh, Hamid Garmani, Zouhair Elhadari, Noureddine Idboufker

An ECC-based authentication protocol designed for machine-to-cloud IoT environments with improved resistance to impersonation and replay attacks.

Contribution. We enhance ECC-based authentication with stronger verification flows for M2C IoT deployments.

Highlights. The protocol improves security while maintaining low computation overhead.

IAENG International Journal of Computer Science · 2025

A Secure Data Storage Model for Wearable Medical IoT Devices Using Blockchain Technology

Zouhair Elhadari, Hicham Zougagh, Noureddine Idboufker, Mohamed Ech-Chebaby, Soufiane Elouaham

We propose a blockchain-based storage model for wearable medical IoT data, emphasizing integrity, auditability, and secure access control.

Contribution. The model provides a secure storage layer tailored to wearable healthcare data and supports verifiable access.

Highlights. The architecture addresses integrity and privacy while remaining feasible for constrained devices.

IAENG International Journal of Computer Science · 2025

Survey on the Adoption of Blockchain Technology in Internet of Things Environments: Techniques, Challenges and Future Research Directions

Zouhair Elhadari, Hicham Zougagh, Noureddine Idboufker, Mohamed Ech-Chebaby

A comprehensive survey of blockchain adoption in IoT systems, highlighting integration techniques, security challenges, and open research problems for scalable deployments.

Contribution. We synthesize deployment patterns and security trade-offs for blockchain-enabled IoT architectures.

Highlights. The survey outlines practical constraints and future directions for privacy-preserving IoT systems.

International Journal of Advanced Computer Science and Applications · 2025

A Secure Authentication Protocol for IoT Devices

Mohamed Ech-Chebaby, Hicham Zougagh, Hamid Garmani, Zouhair Elhadari

This work presents a lightweight authentication protocol tailored to constrained IoT devices. The protocol strengthens mutual authentication and resists replay and impersonation attacks while preserving computational efficiency.

Contribution. We propose a secure authentication protocol for IoT deployments that balances cryptographic strength with device constraints.

Highlights. The protocol is analyzed against common attack vectors and demonstrates improved security assurance with practical deployment costs.

DOI: 10.14569/ijacsa.2025.01608104
