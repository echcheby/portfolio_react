IoT Security: Authentication and Authorization

2023–present

IoT devices require secure, lightweight authentication and authorization mechanisms that can operate under resource constraints.

Methodology. The project focuses on cryptographic protocol design, formal threat modeling, and lightweight validation for resource-constrained devices.

Status. Protocol evaluation and comparative analysis are ongoing, with emphasis on deployability in healthcare IoT contexts.
